About Sayan Mahata

Sayan Mahata (also known as Sayan The Researcher) is a System Architect and Security Researcher from West Bengal, India. He is the Founder of Sayanox Private Limited and the creator of the original Sayanox programming language (file extension .sa).

His work spans compilers, cybersecurity, OSINT, digital forensics and developer tooling across 80+ public repositories on GitHub under the username sayan9168.
