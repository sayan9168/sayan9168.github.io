About Sayan Mahata

Sayan Mahata · Sayan The Researcher

Founder of Sayanox · Sayan The Researcher

Sayan Mahata is a System Architect and Security Researcher from West Bengal, India. Online he is widely known as Sayan The Researcher and by the GitHub handle sayan9168.

He is the Founder of Sayanox Private Limited and the creator of the original Sayanox programming language (file extension .sa). His work spans compilers, cybersecurity, OSINT, digital forensics and developer tooling across 80+ public repositories.
